About Us

ई-समीक्षा | e SamikSha - Quality control monitoring tool is developed to serve as a centralized digital compliance monitoring system for the CISF Aviation Security Group.

Quality control monitoring tool aims to track and report on audit findings and compliance level related to internal CISF Audits, BCAS (Bureau of Civil Aviation Security) Audits,IQCU and ICAO (International Civil Aviation Organization) Audits across all CISF-managed airports.

Features

Audit Status Tracking
Compliance Point Monitoring
Quality Scorecard Generation
Immediate Monitoring
Customizable Settings
Mobile-Friendly Interface
