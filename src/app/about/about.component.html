The QCMT is a centralized digital compliance and audit monitoring platform developed for the CISF Aviation Security Group. The system enables real-time tracking, monitoring, and reporting of audit findings across all CISF-managed airports.

The platform supports audits conducted by Internal CISF teams, the Bureau of Civil Aviation Security (BCAS),IQCU and the International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO). By digitizing the complete audit lifecycle, the system eliminates manual inefficiencies and ensures uniform compliance with national and international aviation security standards.

The system focuses on identifying critical non-compliance areas such as manpower deployment, infrastructure readiness, equipment availability, and personnel training. Interactive dashboards, automated quality scorecards, and real-time status tracking empower command-level officers to take timely, data-driven decisions.
