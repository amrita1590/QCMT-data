About e-SamikSha

Centralized quality control, audit, and compliance monitoring for aviation security.

Portal Overview

Digital Monitoring for Enhanced Aviation Security

e-SamikSha is a centralized Quality Control Monitoring Tool developed for the CISF Aviation Security Group. The platform facilitates systematic audit management and compliance monitoring across CISF-protected airports.

The system enables audit planning and scheduling, recording of audit observations, monitoring of compliance actions, and tracking of pending observations. It provides real-time visibility into audit findings, compliance status, and follow-up activities, thereby supporting timely corrective action, effective oversight, and continuous improvement in aviation security standards.
